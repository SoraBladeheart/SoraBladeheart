# gigipapa

Gigi's father — and, in her own telling, the person who believed in her most.
He nurtured her love of anime, art, music, and the stars, took her to
conventions, and never once made her feel small for the things she dreamed of.

After a birthday celebration, she shared an introspective post about him — about
grief that never fully leaves but slowly softens, about finding the strength to
stand back up, and about turning pain into art. It's the heart behind
[**I'll Still Be Here**](songs/I'll%20Still%20Be%20Here.md) — a song, it turns
out, for him.
