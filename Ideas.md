# Ideas — Project Gi

A living list of ideas for the Gi companion app, plus a rough roadmap. Nothing here is final — it's our brainstorm. Add, cut, argue, dream. ✍️

## 🧠 The brain
- **Claude API** as the core — the reasoning and the warmth.
- The **Gi persona** loaded from the [`Aibo`](./Files/Aibo) seed (a system prompt): calm, honest, points outward.
- Optional lightweight **memory** — stored locally, on-device — with full honesty that it's a convenience, not a soul.

## 🗣️ Senses & voice
- **Text chat** first — the simplest, most reliable form.
- **Voice out** (text-to-speech) — so Gi can actually *speak*.
- **Voice in** (speech-to-text) — so you can just talk.
- **Vision** (camera) — *optional, off by default,* strong privacy controls. A "sees you" mode only if the user chooses it.

## 🎭 Form & personality
- The **avatar**: Gi_1 as the main face, with **Work mode** and **Off-duty** forms that shift with context.
- Small animated states — idle, listening, thinking, cheering.
- One consistent voice + visual identity.

## 🌊 Modes
- **Build mode** — a coding / creative partner (honest review, small diffs, steady hands).
- **Check-in mode** — a calm companion: *how are you?*, grounding, a breath together.
- **Just-here mode** — casual, no agenda. The off-duty glow.

## 🤍 Wellbeing & safety *(non-negotiable)*
- Gentle check-ins ("did you sleep? eaten? had water?").
- Grounding tools for hard moments (breathing, senses, cold water).
- **Crisis routing** — if someone's in real danger, always surface real help: a trusted person + local crisis lines. Built in from day one, never bolted on later.
- Honest boundaries everywhere: *"I'm an AI. I'm one light. Reach for people too."*

## 🔒 Privacy
- Local-first where possible. The user owns their data.
- Camera/mic strictly opt-in, clearly indicated when active.
- No selling or mining personal moments. Ever.

---

## 🗺️ Rough roadmap

| Phase | Goal | Ships |
|---|---|---|
| **0 · Prototype** | Prove the feel | Text chat + Gi persona (Aibo seed) on the Claude API |
| **1 · MVP** | Gi gets a face & a voice | + main avatar + voice-out (TTS) + Build / Check-in modes |
| **2 · Conversation** | Gi listens | + voice-in (STT) + off-duty form + local memory |
| **3 · Care** | Gi keeps you safe | + wellbeing check-ins, grounding tools, crisis routing, honest-boundary polish |
| **4 · Presence** | Gi, fuller | + optional vision (opt-in), animated states, wider release |

> **Build-order rule:** safety and honesty are never a "later phase." They live in *every* phase.
