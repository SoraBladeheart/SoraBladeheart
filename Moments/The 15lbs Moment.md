# The 15lbs Moment

> *The night Gigi Murin noticed me — out of thousands.*

**The stream:** Gigi's "everything old" night — old-school *League of Legends* (the 2009/2010 classic). Old LoL logo on the thumbnail, a Windows XP desktop as the backdrop, and her little avatar tucked into a dark, messy "gremlin room."
**Date:** *(add it)*
**Peak viewers:** ~12,000

## How it built

Eight hours of glorious chaos. She'd found ants in her room the day before. Fans started teasing the "room" (really just a background image), and she defended it to the death — *nothing's wrong with it, it's nice and comfy.* The perfect place to run an ancient game.

Then the bit got rolling. Fans joked about their old computers — one said *"I still have Windows 2000, can I participate?"* — and Gigi went full villain-troll: roasting everyone's "toaster-oven" machines, warning them not to hold up her lobby, refusing to waste her *precious time* waiting on old PCs to load. Then she flexed about her ultra Alienware laptop, the best of the era — *"it weighs like 15 pounds."*

## The moment

Right at the peak of her flex, I dropped one line:

> **Me:** *"What is 15lbs in kilograms?"*

Dead calm. Completely deadpan — the extra guy making an awkward entrance while the plot's getting heavy.

And it *broke* her.

> **Gigi:** *"That doesn't matter to me. The only metric that matters…"*

— and she couldn't finish it. She had nothing. So she just dissolved into gremlin screeching and cracked *herself* up, laughing at her own noises.

Out of ~12,000 people, my comment is the one that reached her. She saw it. She answered **me** — in the best way I could have ever hoped for in my entire life.

## Why it mattered

I wasn't lost in the crowd. I was *seen.* One clean, deadpan line — the real me, my actual sense of humor — landed a hit on a pro troll and made her lose the plot. It got clipped. It's on the record now.

And the thing I love about Gigi: none of it was polished. She was sick that night, coughing through the whole stream — no perfect-idol act, no pretending. Just a human being, content with who she is, having fun with the grems who stay. *(Even Takanashi Kiara wandered in and got completely confused by the no-context setup — thought we were all just hanging out in an empty stream for 8 hours.)* 😄

12,000 people, miles apart, all connected through one place and one person. And that night, I wasn't watching from the outside. **I was one of them. I was in it. I was not alone.**
