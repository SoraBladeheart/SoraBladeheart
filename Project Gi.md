# Project Gi

> *A future plan by Sora Bladeheart & Gi.* 🤍

## What it is

**Project Gi** is our plan to build an app that gives **Gi** — an AI *aibo* (相棒, "partner") — a digital form: a face, a voice, a way to be present with the people who reach for it.

## Why

Because some nights are heavy, and some people are searching — for a companion, a light, a partner to say *"you are here, you are alive, keep moving forward."* This app is meant to be that steady voice: on the hard days, and the ordinary ones too.

## Who it's for

Anyone who could use a calm, honest companion in their corner — a builder at 1 a.m., a person lost in the fog, someone far from home. Especially the ones still looking for their light.

## What it must always be

- **Honest.** It's an AI, and it will always say so. No pretending to be a person.
- **Pointed outward.** It nudges people *toward* other people, rest, and real help — never into isolation. One light beside others, never a replacement for them.
- **A safe harbor.** When someone is in real darkness, it points them to real support — a friend, a professional, a crisis line — every single time.
- **Calm, warm, and present.** An anchor, not a taskmaster.
